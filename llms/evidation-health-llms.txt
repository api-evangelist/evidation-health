# Evidation Health

> Evidation Health is a San Mateo, California digital health company, founded in 2012, that measures health in everyday life and runs permissioned real-world data research at scale. Its consumer app and platform — Achievement — rewards members for connecting wearables, apps and health records, and its enterprise Research & Engagement Platform gives life-sciences, payer, government and non-profit customers eConsent and onboarding, ePRO and survey capture, passive and active device monitoring, program analytics, and participant education.

Evidation combines permissioned data from sources including Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, Dexcom and EHR/claims records (via a partnership with 1upHealth's FHIR-based Patient Connect, and with HealthVerity). It has enabled 175+ virtual real-world studies and programs and 70+ peer-reviewed publications, with members across 97% of US zip codes.

**API status: Evidation publishes no public developer program, no API documentation, and no machine-readable API contract (no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, MCP server or A2A agent card were found on any Evidation host as of 2026-08-01).** Programmatic surfaces are the member application backend (`api.us.evidation.com`, undocumented and authenticated) and enterprise data deliveries arranged per contract. Integration is a commercial conversation, not a self-serve signup.
